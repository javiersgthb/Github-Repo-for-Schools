Lesson 1: Catalysts for Change

Lesson 1 begins with a brief preview of the pros and cons of new technologies before providing you with an extensive introduction to milestones in computing, networking, and information storage and retrieval. You will also receive a brief introduction to the concepts of cloud computing and cloud storage.

Learning Objectives

  • Objective 1 Identify criteria for determining the positive and negative impacts of new technologies.
  • Objective 2 Describe milestones in computing, including increasingly sophisticated calculating devices, the advent of programming languages and time-sharing, microprocessors, and personal computers.
  • Objective 3 Outline milestones in networking, including the telegraph, the telephone, radio, television, ARPNET, email, the Internet, broadband, and wireless networks.
  • Objective 4 Discuss milestones in information storage and retrieval from the Greek alphabet to the advent of search engines.
