The Era of the Republicans

With the election of Abraham Lincoln in 1860, the Republican Party went from a relatively new and minor party to a major political party. From 1860–1932, the Republican Party controlled the White House and dominated congressional elections, with the exceptions of the Grover Cleveland and Woodrow Wilson administrations. The Republican Party was supported by business and financial interests yet also was instrumental in freeing African Americans from slavery.

Notable aspects of this era include the following:

  • The size and scope of big business expanded greatly.
  • The western frontier of the North American continent was closed.
  • Both Black people and women gained the right to vote.
