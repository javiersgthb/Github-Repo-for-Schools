Party Platforms

Every four years, the national parties get together at their conventions and come up with a platform. A platform is a list of stances on issues that are then presented to Americans as the party’s vision of what should be done if the party gains power in government. These platforms are rarely static for long periods of time. As Americans’ attitudes change, the major parties will change to better position themselves to gain voters. A plank is a party’s stance on an individual issue.

Republicans Democrats

Economic Policy

Support less government interference when it comes to Americans’ economic lives:

  • Less business regulation
  • Lower taxes
  • Less government spending

Support more government intervention in Americans’ economic lives: 

  • More business regulation
  • Large government programs
  • Potentially higher taxes to pay for programs

Social Policy

Support more government intervention in issues such as:

  • Abortion
  • Drug use
  • Police powers

Support less government intervention; likely to support:

  • The pro-choice rights of women
  • Certain personal-freedom issues, such as the legalization of marijuana
  • Curtailing police powers
