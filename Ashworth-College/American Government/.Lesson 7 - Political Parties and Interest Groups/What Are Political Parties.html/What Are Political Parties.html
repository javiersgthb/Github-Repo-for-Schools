What Are Political Parties?

A political party is a group of people who share the same general political goal, working together to seek public office and use that power to accomplish their goals. In a way, it can be said that Washington was overly hopeful in his warnings about the evils of political parties. It makes sense that when elected positions grant power to a person, people would seek out those positions. It also makes sense that people are more effective when they band together to achieve their mutual goals.

One thing to always remember about major American political parties is that they’re election-oriented rather than ideologically driven. In other words, they ultimately care about holding onto power. Parties may modify their stances on particular issues if they perceive that doing so will increase their chances of winning elections. This is evidenced by the changing stances and changing emphasis on particular issues throughout the years. The Democratic and Republican parties of today are very different from those same parties of 40 years ago.
