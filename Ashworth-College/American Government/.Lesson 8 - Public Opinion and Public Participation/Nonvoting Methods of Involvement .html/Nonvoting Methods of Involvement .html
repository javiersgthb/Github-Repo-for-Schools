Nonvoting Methods of Involvement

Voting isn’t the only way for Americans to get involved in government. In many local governments across the United States, official positions don’t pay a salary, and volunteers must step up to take care of the organizational needs of the community. Citizens may donate their time and money to help like-minded people take office. Many volunteer to work phone banks for candidates or will canvass—go door to door trying to sway voters regarding issues and candidates. In many ways, voting is the minimum amount of involvement when it comes to influencing government.
