Powers Shared by National and State Governments

Both state and national level of governments share the power to

  • Collect taxes
  • Build and maintain roads
  • Try criminals in court

All levels have the power to tax people living and doing business within the country and respective states. Americans find themselves paying local, then state, and then federal taxes. All levels of government are involved in building and maintaining the nation’s roads, and all have the ability to try criminals in court. Where there’s conflict between state and federal law, the uncertainty is settled in the court system with courts deciding on interpretation of the Constitution.
