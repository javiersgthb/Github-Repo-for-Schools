First Amendment Freedoms

Perhaps the most recognized and potentially the most controversial amendment to the Constitution would be the First Amendment, which comprises the freedoms of

  • Speech
  • Religion
  • Press
  • Petition
  • Assembly 

Keep in mind that this amendment protects individuals and organizations from government interference, but not from other private individuals and groups. The government can’t arrest someone for saying something hateful in a video and then sharing that video online. A private company, however, can refuse to hire such a person, and private individuals may choose to not associate with that person. In this case, while Americans have freedom of speech as far as the government is concerned, Americans aren’t free of private consequences.
