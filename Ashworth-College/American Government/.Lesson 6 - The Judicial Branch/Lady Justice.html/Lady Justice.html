Lady Justice

The Supreme Court symbolizes American justice. Today, many countries around the world, including the United States, use Lady Justice as a symbol for their courts. Courts and law schools may depict her in statues on their premises or as an image in their emblems.

Select each item to learn more.

Blindfold

Some, but not all, depictions of Lady Justice show her wearing a blindfold. The blindfold implies that justice is blind and does not judge based on appearance.

Scale

The scale represents weighing facts and evidence to decide a verdict. It also shows the duty of justice to restore balance to society.

Sword

The sword shows authority, including the power to punish injustice. It also implies being able to cut through obstacles to get to the facts of a case.
